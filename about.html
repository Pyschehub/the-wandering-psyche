About MINDSCAPE

A student-led psychology publication for curious minds.

MINDSCAPE was created by two psychology students who believed that writing about the mind could help others understand themselves. Our mission is to explore human behavior, emotions, identity, and relationships through evidence-based essays, original research, book reviews, and honest reflection. We believe psychology should feel accessible — like recognition, not instruction — and that the best insights come from asking genuine questions about everyday experience.

We publish work that bridges the gap between academic psychology and the real questions young adults are asking about their inner lives. Whether through our research column, our anonymous Dear Mind advice series, or our long-form essays, every piece is written with intellectual curiosity, empathy, and a commitment to psychological rigor.

Co-founder & Editor-in-Chief

Maya Chen

Third-year psychology student with a focus on developmental and social psychology. Maya started MINDSCAPE out of frustration with academic writing that felt inaccessible. She writes primarily about identity, emotion, and the social self — and believes the best psychology writing should feel like recognition, not instruction.

Co-founder & Research Lead

Leila Nassir

Final-year psychology and cognitive science student specializing in behavioral research and mental health communication. Leila leads MINDSCAPE's research column and oversees the Dear Mind column. She's passionate about bridging the gap between empirical findings and the questions real people are asking about their inner lives.
