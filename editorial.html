Why Young Adults Feel Uncertain About the Future

October 2024 · Maya Chen

In an era of unprecedented choice and constant comparison, the paralysis of decision-making has become a defining experience for many young adults. Drawing from Schwartz's paradox of choice and recent research on emerging adulthood, this editorial examines why "what do you want to do with your life?" has become such a loaded question — and how psychology can help us reframe it.
