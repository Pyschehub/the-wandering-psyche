Social Media Use and Self-Esteem in University Students

"Does the frequency and type of social media platform used correlate with reported self-esteem levels among undergraduates aged 18–25?"

Methodology: Mixed-methods approach combining a 22-item online survey (adapted Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale) with brief qualitative interviews (n=34). Participants recruited through campus channels across two universities. Platform usage tracked via self-report over a two-week period.

Perfectionism and Academic Burnout

"How do maladaptive perfectionism traits correlate with academic burnout among university students during examination periods?"

Methodology: Online survey (n=247) using Frost Multidimensional Perfectionism Scale and Maslach Burnout Inventory-Student Survey. Data collected across three time points during a semester.

Sleep Quality and Emotional Regulation in Young Adults

"Is there a significant relationship between self-reported sleep quality and emotional regulation strategies in adults aged 18-30?"

Methodology: Correlational design using Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index and Emotion Regulation Questionnaire. Online distribution with n=156 participants.
